Lamination doors & the Door Studio — FAQ

How can I see a lamination door design on my own wall?

Open the Sparsh 360 Door Studio, take or upload a photo of your wall, drag the four dots onto the corners of the door frame, then tap any design. The door is shown on your wall in the right perspective. It is free and works in your phone browser without any app.

How many lamination door designs does Sparsh 360 have?

Over 1,100 lamination door designs from Aastha, Ramdev, Deluxe and Wonder Sign. Every design has a code, for example RD-114 or WL-DG-1026, that you can quote when you enquire. Browse all designs.

What is a lamination door?

A lamination door is a door finished with a decorative laminate sheet printed with wood grains, patterns or artwork. Laminated doors are durable, easy to clean, resist moisture and scratches better than paint, and come in hundreds of designs at an affordable price.

What is the price of a lamination door?

The price depends on the design, door size and finish. Choose a design in the Door Studio and send it to Sparsh 360 on WhatsApp at +91 70435 88033 to get the exact price, sizes and delivery time.

How do I order a door I like?

Tap Share on WhatsApp in the Door Studio to send the picture of the door on your wall with its design code, or tap Chat with us. The Sparsh 360 team will reply with price and availability, and you can also see it in our showroom in Vadodara.

Do I need to install an app or sign up?
